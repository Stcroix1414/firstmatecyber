🚩 The Red Flags Card

If you see any of these — stop and check first.

  1. They called, emailed, or texted you first
  2. They want gift cards, wire transfer, or crypto
  3. They say act now — no time to think
  4. They say keep it a secret
  5. You feel rushed, scared, or unsure

How First Mate Cyber Works

No apps to learn. No accounts to manage. If you can send an email, you can use First Mate Cyber.

1. Something looks off

A strange email, a text about a package, a pop-up on your computer, a call about your grandson. Don't click. Don't pay. Don't panic.

2. Send it to us

Email a photo of it, forward the message, and describe what happened. A real local person — not a call center — looks at it the same day.

3. Get a straight answer

"It's a scam — delete it," or "it's real, here's what to do." And if something already went wrong, we clean it up and secure your accounts.
